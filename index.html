Weight Trajectory Calculator

Estimates your path to a goal date by recomputing BMR and TDEE every single day as your weight changes — so the burn rate slows down naturally as you get lighter, the way real metabolism does.

Enter your details and hit Calculate trajectory to see your day-by-day projection.

Method: BMR is computed daily with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation from that day's simulated weight, then multiplied by your activity factor for TDEE. Each day's deficit (TDEE − calories eaten) is converted to body-mass change at 7,700 kcal per kg (≈3,500 kcal per lb) of fat, so the daily loss amount shrinks as your weight — and therefore your BMR — drops.

This is a mathematical estimate, not medical advice. It assumes constant calorie intake and activity level, and doesn't account for water-weight fluctuation, metabolic adaptation beyond BMR scaling, muscle gain, or lean-mass changes. Consult a doctor or dietitian before starting any weight-loss plan, especially at aggressive deficits.
